### Post 1 (Root + Original Infographic)
Nothing kills a crypto transfer faster than getting stuck with zero native gas.

You hold USDT or USDC, hit Send, and get blocked because you lack SOL or TRX.

@TokenPocket_TP solves both on Solana and TRON.

Here is how their transfer features work in practice 🧵👇

### Post 2
1/ Solana: 3 Daily Gas-Free Transfers

On TokenPocket (Android v2.9.0+ / iOS v2.11.0+), every Solana account gets 3 free token transfers per day.

• Covers SPL tokens like USDT & USDC (excludes native $SOL)
• Instant settlement, $0 gas fee inside the daily quota

### Post 3
2/ How to use Solana Gas-Free in 3 clicks:

• Open your Solana wallet in TokenPocket
• Pick an SPL token (e.g. USDT or USDC) and tap Send
• Keep the fee option set (SOL, USDT, or USDC all show $0 within your 3 daily free sends)

No emergency SOL top-ups needed.

### Post 4
3/ TRON: Pay Network Fees With USDT or USDD (Zero TRX)

Sending USDT on TRON normally consumes Energy & Bandwidth. Without TRX, your funds get stranded.

In TokenPocket's Transfer screen, tap "Pay With" and switch from TRX to USDT or USDD — send immediately with zero TRX.

### Post 5
4/ Cut TRON transfer costs even further:

• Energy Rental: rent Energy instead of burning TRX on smart contract calls
• Daily Subsidies: claim 3,240 daily subsidized transfers (first-come, first-served) on the transfer page
• Network Fee Redemption Codes for extra savings
